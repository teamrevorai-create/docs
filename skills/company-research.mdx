The Revor Company Research Skill prepares a sourced company background report. It confirms public company identity, adds customs trade evidence when the entity match is reliable, and finds relevant contacts from a verified domain.
## Install in Codex
Run this in the project where you want to use the Skill:
```bash
npx skills add laiye-revor/revor-skills --skill revor-company-research --agent codex
```
Check `/skills` and invoke `$revor-company-research`, or describe a matching research task. Keep the complete Skill folder, including its scripts.
## When to use it
- Prepare for a meeting or company background check.
- Assess a supplier, buyer, or customer.
- Review trade activity and relevant decision-maker contacts.
## Research flow
The Skill identifies the official company and domain, selects the appropriate customs role and catalog, verifies a candidate before a paid report, and searches contacts once using the verified employee domain. Important claims retain source links, dates, and scope.
It uses Revor MCP tools when connected. Without MCP, its bundled client requires a Revor API key. The Skill does not perform outreach without a separate request.
