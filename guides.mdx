## Why use Revor AI

Revor brings prospect discovery, research, engagement, and qualification into one workflow.

- **Find buyers with real-time intent.** Describe your ideal customer profile and discover matching companies and decision-makers that are ready to buy.
- **Understand every account.** Use Company Deep Research for business context and Company Enrichment to find the right contacts.
- **Engage across every channel.** Reach prospects with personalized, multi-step sequences over email, LinkedIn, and WhatsApp, and keep every reply and follow-up in one history.
- **Qualify inbound leads automatically.** Answer inquiries, collect the details your team needs, and hand off sales-ready leads based on your rules.
- **Tap into trade data.** Research importers, exporters, and customs activity when it matters for your target market.
- **Work where you already work.** Use Revor natively in ChatGPT and Codex, or in any AI client through MCP.

## Security and privacy

Your data and your prospects' data are protected at every step.

- **Access control:** Role-based permissions for your team. Connected AI clients use only the permissions you grant, and you can revoke them anytime.
- **Encryption:** TLS 1.2 or 1.3 for data in transit, and AES-256-GCM for stored credentials and OAuth tokens.
- **Reliability:** Daily automated backups with integrity checks, plus rate limits and activity monitoring.
- **Privacy:** Clear rules for how personal data is collected, used, retained, and deleted, including data from connected Google accounts.
