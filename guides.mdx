## Why use Revor AI

Revor brings prospect discovery, research, and engagement into one workflow. You can identify companies that match your target market, understand an account before reaching out, and use that context to make conversations more relevant. Connected channels help you manage follow-ups and replies without losing the history of the conversation.

For inbound work, an agent can receive inquiries, collect the information your team needs, and hand off conversations for human follow-up.

## Who Revor AI is for

- **Founders and small teams** building a repeatable way to find and engage potential customers.
- **Outbound sales teams** researching accounts and reaching decision-makers across channels.
- **Inbound growth teams** qualifying inquiries and passing promising conversations to a person.
- **Talent sourcing teams** finding and engaging candidates.

## What can you do with Revor AI

- **Discover prospects:** Describe an ideal customer profile and find matching companies and decision-makers with Target.
- **Understand each account:** Use Company Deep Research to review business context and Company Enrichment to find relevant contacts.
- **Run outreach:** Prepare an Agent using your company information and goals, then connect channels for personalized messages and follow-ups.
- **Qualify inbound inquiries:** Configure an agent to answer questions, collect customer information, and set handoff rules.
- **Work with trade data:** Use Revor's MCP tools to research importers, exporters, and customs activity when that information is relevant to your target market.

## Integrate Revor AI into your workflow

Use **Connect** to link the channels where you already talk with prospects, including email, LinkedIn, and WhatsApp. If you work from an AI client, [connect Revor with MCP](/mcp/overview) to request discovery, research, contact search, and authorized outreach in natural language. You can also browse the [MCP tools](/mcp/tools) and Agent Skills for [company discovery](/mcp/skills/company-discovery), [company research](/mcp/skills/company-research), and [contact search](/mcp/skills/contact-search).

## Security, privacy, and compliance

Revor AI uses access controls, encryption, backups, and monitoring measures designed to protect data and support platform reliability. The controls below describe their specific scope.

### Account access and permissions

- **Sign-in and teams:** Sign in with email and password, reset a password, and manage team members through invitations and removal.
- **Role-based access:** Roles control access to administrative and business APIs. Open API access supports granular permissions.
- **Connected AI clients:** An MCP client uses the permissions of the Revor account that authorized it. You can revoke an OAuth grant or rotate an API key to end that access.

### Encryption and data protection

- **Data in transit:** Network communications use TLS 1.2 or TLS 1.3.
- **Sensitive credentials at rest:** Stored sensitive credentials and OAuth tokens use AES-256-GCM encryption. Encryption keys are managed separately from application code.
- **Data access and recovery:** User-scoped access controls limit data access. Automated database backups run daily and include integrity checks.

### Platform safeguards

- **API protection:** Selected high-risk API endpoints have rate limits.
- **Activity records:** Structured access logs and task event tracking support monitoring. Application logs are rotated and compressed.
- **Registration controls:** Email, domain, and IP address signals are used for registration risk controls.
