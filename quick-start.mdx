## Before you start

Each of the following comes up during setup. Prepare them first so you can go through the steps without stopping.

- **A Revor AI account**<br /> [https://revor.ai/auth/signin](https://revor.ai/auth/signin)
- **At least one connected channel**<br />Your AI employee reaches people through the channels you connect, such as email, LinkedIn, or WhatsApp. <u>Learn more about how to connect channels.</u>
- **Your company information and goals**<br />- Your company website.<br />- The LinkedIn profile of the person your AI employee represents.<br />- Your BD goals.
- **Credits on your account**<br /><u>See Credits and Plans</u>

## Revor AI employees

In Revor, an AI employee is an agent you train on your company, products, and goals to handle part of your sales work. You can train as many AI employees as you need, and each one has its own knowledge, goals, and connected channels. There are two types of AI employees:

<Frame>
  ![Image](/images/image.png "Image")
</Frame>

- **Outbound AI employee** (lead generation): finds potential buyers and reaches out to them across your connected channels.
- **Inbound AI employee** (lead qualification): answers incoming inquiries, collects customer information, and hands promising conversations to your team.
