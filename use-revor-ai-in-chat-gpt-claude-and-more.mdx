## Before you start

- **A Revor AI account**<br /> [https://revor.ai/auth/signin](https://revor.ai/auth/signin)
- **An AI client that supports plugins or remote MCP**<br />For example, ChatGPT, Codex, or Claude.
- **Credits on your account**<br />Make sure you have credits available.

## Connect Revor AI

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Official Plugin">
    Revor AI is available as an official plugin in ChatGPT and Codex, so you can add it without entering a server URL.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Find and add the plugin">
        In ChatGPT, open the plugin directory and search for **Revor AI**. Select the plugin, then click **Install**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Authorize on [revor.ai](http://revor.ai)">
        Sign up or sign in on revor.ai, check the account and requested permissions, then click **Allow access**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Verify the connection">
        Ask ChatGPT or Codex to call `revor_whoami`. It confirms which Revor account is connected.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Custom Connector">
    In other AI clients that support remote MCP, such as Claude, add Revor AI as a custom connector.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Ask your AI client to install Revor AI">
        Send this message to your AI client:

        `Install Revor AI MCP by following https://revor.ai/docs/mcp/install`

        Your AI client reads the installation guide and sets up Revor AI for you.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Authorize on [revor.ai](http://revor.ai)">
        In your AI client, find the custom connector you just added and click to authorize it. Depending on what your client supports, you'll either:

        - Authorize in the browser<br />you're taken to revor.ai. Sign up or sign in, check the account and requested permissions, then click **Allow access**.
        - Enter an API key<br />create one at [Revor AI API Keys](https://revor.ai/my-api-keys), then enter it in the field your client provides.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Verify the connection">
        Ask the AI client to call `revor_whoami`. It confirms which Revor account is connected.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Try it out

Ask your AI client in plain language, for example:

- Find 10 pet supply retail chains in the US, prioritizing companies that have recently posted job openings for purchasing or buyer roles.
- Can you research Petco for me? What opportunities are there, and how should we approach them?
- Find the purchasing managers at [herweck.de](http://herweck.de).
- Draft a personalized outreach message for each key contact you just found.

Revor sends outreach only through your authorized connected accounts and with your confirmation.

<Check>
  You can now use Revor AI right inside your AI client to find prospects, research companies, and reach out, without leaving the chat.
</Check>
