# Product brief

## Description

REVOR AI helps people discover prospects, research companies, find business contacts, and inspect trade activity. Its remote MCP service makes these tools available in compatible AI clients.

## Primary audience

People using REVOR AI for prospecting and research, including those who want to connect it to an MCP-compatible AI client. This audience is inferred from the existing documentation.

## Jobs to be done

- Start working in the REVOR AI app.
- Connect an AI client to the REVOR AI MCP server and verify the connection.
- Find the relevant tool or Agent Skill for a prospecting or research workflow.

## Motivation

The guides and MCP reference provide one place to discover REVOR AI workflows and connect its tools. This description is inferred from the current pages; product positioning should be confirmed with the REVOR AI team before expanding the guides.
